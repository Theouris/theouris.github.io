01

Myasthenia gravis

In typical AChR-antibody–positive MG, antibodies reduce functional postsynaptic ACh receptors. Less efficient transmission produces fatigable weakness. AChE inhibition gives released ACh more time to activate remaining receptors; it improves symptoms without removing the antibodies. [7]

02

Bowel & bladder

More ACh at muscarinic receptors increases gut motility and detrusor contraction, helping bowel propulsion and bladder emptying. Neostigmine is used in selected nonobstructive settings, particularly acute colonic pseudo-obstruction; it is not routine treatment for every postoperative ileus or urinary retention. [1]

Exclude mechanical obstruction before using a pro-motility approach. [9]

Organophosphorus insecticides and nerve agents inhibit AChE. Malathion is one example; metabolic activation produces malaoxon, a potent cholinesterase inhibitor. [3] [10]

Sustained ACh excess can impair neuromuscular transmission through persistent depolarization and receptor desensitization. More transmitter no longer means more useful contraction. [7]

Respiratory failure can combine airway secretions, bronchospasm, respiratory muscle weakness, and central depression. CNS toxicity may include seizures. [3]

Organophosphate poisoning also requires urgent airway support and decontamination. [3]

RECEPTOR BLOCKADE

Atropine

A competitive muscarinic antagonist that counters secretions, bronchospasm, and bradycardia. It does not reverse nicotinic skeletal muscle paralysis. [3]

ENZYME REACTIVATION

Pralidoxime 2-PAM

Removes the organophosphate-derived phosphoryl group from inhibited AChE before aging. It can improve peripheral muscarinic and nicotinic effects, especially weakness, and is given with atropine. Timing and the specific agent matter; CNS penetration is limited. [4]

ANESTHESIA CONNECTION

Succinylcholine

A rapid-onset, short-duration depolarizing neuromuscular blocker used to facilitate intubation and skeletal muscle relaxation during anesthesia. Succinylcholine can trigger a hypermetabolic skeletal muscle crisis (malignant hyperthermia) in susceptible individuals. This is distinct from cholinergic toxicity; stop triggering agents and treat urgently, including dantrolene. [5]
