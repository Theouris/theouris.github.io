A

Odd or eccentric

Cluster A

Odd or eccentric

Unusual thinking or behavior, distrust, and social detachment can make close relationships difficult.

Paranoid personality disorder
Persistent distrust. A person may be on guard and interpret innocent remarks as threats.
Schizoid personality disorder
Little desire for close relationships, limited outward emotion, and indifference to praise or criticism.
Schizotypal personality disorder
Discomfort with close relationships, unusual beliefs or magical thinking, and eccentric behavior or appearance. It is considered part of the schizophrenia spectrum.

Dramatic or erratic

Cluster B

Dramatic or erratic

Intense emotions, impulsivity, or dramatic behavior can strain relationships.

Antisocial personality disorder
Repeated disregard for others’ rights, such as deceit or aggression. Diagnosis requires age 18 or older and evidence of conduct disorder before age 15.
Borderline personality disorder
Unstable moods, self-image, and relationships. Emptiness, splitting (all-or-nothing views of others), self-harm, and suicidal thoughts can occur. Dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) can help.
Histrionic personality disorder
Persistent attention seeking and expressive, rapidly shifting emotions. Behavior can be theatrical or inappropriately flirtatious.
Narcissistic personality disorder
Grandiosity, entitlement, a need for admiration, and limited empathy. Criticism can trigger anger.

Anxious or fearful

Cluster C

Anxious or fearful

Anxiety and fear can show up as social avoidance, reliance on others, or a strong need for control.

Avoidant personality disorder
A person wants close relationships but holds back because they feel inadequate or fear criticism and rejection.
Obsessive-compulsive personality disorder (OCPD)
Rigid perfectionism and a need for order and control. These standards often feel right to the person (ego-syntonic). OCD commonly involves unwanted obsessions or compulsions.
Dependent personality disorder
A strong need for care and reassurance, difficulty making decisions independently, and fear of separation.
